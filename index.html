What just happened

    Built around the way a dorm actually runs.

    This is the Room Canvas, the first screen you see: live counts on top, every room and bed below.

    A map of your building, floor by floor

    Lay out rooms the way they sit in your hallway, and draw the hallway, stairs, comfort rooms and office as shapes. Click a room to see who is in each bed and when they leave.

    • Separate map for every floor
    • Bed colours update as tenants move in and out
    • Tenant dates and emergency contacts one click away

    See next month before it arrives

    Every bed is counted once, and beds about to free up are marked ahead of time, so you can line up the next tenant before the room goes empty.

    • Upcoming vacancies for the next 7 days
    • Occupancy for the whole dorm and for each room
    • Reminders for check-outs that are due or missed

    Many dorms, one view for head office

    If you run several branches, connect them and watch all of them together. Each branch decides what it shares, and can stop sharing at any time.

    • Live occupancy and vacancies for every branch
    • Safe, moderate and risk levels with history and a forecast
    • A public directory where people can find free beds

    Your marketing agents see today's vacancies. They can't change a thing.

    Give an agent a code and password and they get a live, view-only page of your dorm. When you check someone in or out, their page updates by itself, so they never sell a bed that is already taken.

    The same dorm, a moment later. No names, no buttons.

    Always up to date

    Agents see the same numbers you do, minutes after you change them. No calls to ask what's free.

    Look, don't touch

    Their page has no check-in, check-out or edit buttons. Only you can change your records.

    Tenant privacy kept

    Agents see free and taken beds, room type and floor. They never see names, photos or contact details.

    You stay in control

    See who has access, and remove or block anyone at any time.

    And the everyday things, handled.

    Check-in that catches mistakes

    The system stops a tenant being placed in a room for the wrong gender or group, and won't save a stay without dates.

    Reservations without double-booking

    Hold a specific bed for a client's exact dates. Only beds that are really free for those dates are offered.

    Short stays kept separate

    Temporary guests get their own colour and reminders, so they never get mixed up with regular tenants.

    Tenant records you can trust

    Rate each stay good or bad at check-out. Past tenants stay on file for two years.

    Overdue stays flagged

    A tenant who passes their check-out date turns red and appears in your reminders the same day.

    Help built in

    A guided tutorial on every page and an assistant that answers questions like “which rooms have free beds?”
